EMMS – Enterprise Memo Management System (working prototype)
=============================================================

HOW TO OPEN
  Double-click "start.bat" (or "index.html"). It opens in your web browser (Edge or Chrome).
  Tip: right-click start.bat > Show more options > Send to > Desktop (create shortcut).
  Nothing to install. Works offline.

HOW TO TRY THE FULL WORKFLOW
  1. Memos > All Memos > Create Memo. Fill in the form > Next: Review Memo > Submit for Approval.
  2. Click your name (top right) > "Switch User (demo)" > pick the approver shown on the memo.
  3. My Approvals > Review > Approve. Repeat for each approver level.
  4. Switch back to Juan Dela Cruz > My Releases > Release.
  5. Switch to any employee > My Acknowledgements > Acknowledge.

IMPORTANT – WHERE THE DATA LIVES
  This prototype saves everything inside the browser on THIS computer only.
  Other people will not see your memos, and clearing browser data erases them.
  Use Administration > Backup & Recovery to download a backup file.
  Administration > Backup & Recovery > "Reset to Sample Data" restores the demo data.

FILES
  index.html            – the app
  assets/styles.css     – look & feel (colors, layout)
  assets/data.js        – sample data and workflow rules
  assets/pages-*.js     – the screens
